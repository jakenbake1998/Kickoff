What you get in Premiere
  • A bin for every camera. Named after your camera folders, so “B Cam (FX3)” stays B Cam (FX3), with its own label color.
  • Every clip lined up to the song. Per camera, a Synced sequence with each take on its own track at the right spot, and a Condensed copy packed onto as few tracks as possible for multicam. Takes where the song restarted are split into passes.
  • A Breakup per camera. All of that camera’s clips back to back, with every audio channel, to scrub through.
  • No syncing. Bins, Breakups and an empty Edit sequence, with audio sorted into Music, SFX and Captured.
  • Ready to cut. A 4K Edit sequence, and every clip scaled to fill the frame, so no black edges.
  • Shooting over several days? Run the same folder again and Kickoff adds only the new cards.
